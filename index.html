Two counters, one operator

Freundschaft Associates was built on a simple bet: run a small number of franchise kitchens exceptionally well, rather than a large number adequately.

We operate as a multi-brand franchise partnership out of Bengaluru — holding the counter-to-kitchen operations for Burger Singh and Baskin Robbins outlets, and everything behind them: staffing, supply, compliance, and the books.
